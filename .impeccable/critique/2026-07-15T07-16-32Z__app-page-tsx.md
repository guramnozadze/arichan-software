## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment: FAIL - well-executed, but inside the single most saturated template.** A design-literate visitor clocks this as "AI-era awwwards portfolio" in seconds: grain overlay, Lenis smooth scroll, pinned letter-stagger hero, stats marquee, scroll-scrubbed manifesto, hover-fill work rows, giant display CTA - every beat of the saturated family, in canonical order. Two absolute bans are violated: gradient hero text (globals.css:93-103) and the tiny uppercase tracked eyebrow stamped on all four sections (Hero.tsx:84, Manifesto.tsx:70, Work.tsx:80, Finale.tsx:41). Two of three fonts (Instrument Sans, IBM Plex Mono) are reflex-reject defaults. The one genuinely bespoke element - the extruded 3D "A" monogram (MonogramCanvas.tsx) - is what the rest of the page should be built from.

**Deterministic scan:** CLI scan of app/ + components/ came back clean (0 findings; regex mode only for TSX). The in-page rendered scan found **42 warnings**: ai-color-palette x40 (the 16 hero gradient letter spans, 24 purple-on-dark text elements including the eyebrows, marquee separators, and manifesto highlights), all-caps-body x1 (hero eyebrow, 43 chars uppercase), overused-font x1 (Instrument Sans as primary). The 40 palette hits collapse to ~10 distinct styling decisions (per-letter spans and duplicated marquee copies fan out), but the detector independently agrees with the review's two headline tells: the gradient text and the purple-neon-on-dark eyebrow grammar. ## Overall Impression

This is skilled execution of a borrowed identity. The craft floor is high - focus states, reduced-motion intent, a one-mechanism accent system, a genuinely bespoke 3D monogram - but the page proves fluency in 2025's cinematic-portfolio template rather than a point of view, and it spends ~85% of its scroll distance on choreography while the actual proof of skill (three live products) sits at 75% depth. The single biggest opportunity: make the monogram the identity, cut the template garnish, and get visitors to the work in half the scroll.

## What's Working

1. **The 3D monogram is real craft.** Hand-built extruded "A" geometry with a triangular counter, brand-hue Lightformer environment, damped scroll orbit, and viewport-aware placement (MonogramCanvas.tsx:16-79). The one element that makes a visitor ask "how was this made?"
2. **Accent-per-project is one mechanism with three expressions.** A single --row-accent custom property drives tag, hover fill, and arrow (Work.tsx:93, globals.css:177-228), and :focus-visible gets the identical treatment as hover - keyboard users get the full experience.
3. **Voice discipline.** One display family shouting, sans reading, mono labeling; consistent arrow language (↘ in-page, ↗ external); copy that commits ("I ship products, not slide decks.").

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Reduced-motion users never see the hero.**
- Why it matters: with prefers-reduced-motion, .hero-track collapses to 100vh (globals.css:298-300) but the curtain keeps its -mt-[100vh] (app/page.tsx:16), so the page opens on the marquee; name, monogram, and intro are permanently invisible and the h1 is unreachable. Confirmed in screenshots. Sibling bug: .mani-stage { padding: 8rem 0 } wipes horizontal padding, so manifesto text sits flush at the viewport edge.
- Fix: zero the curtain's negative margin inside the same media query (give the div a class), and restore horizontal padding on .mani-stage.
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

**[P1] Work-row hover fails contrast on the violet row.**
- Why it matters: hover swaps text to ink at 0.72-0.8 alpha over --row-accent; on PlusConverter's #8b30e0 that is roughly 2.7-3.0:1 - the flagship project's tag, pitch, and domain go illegible at the exact moment of engagement (WCAG AA needs 4.5:1).
- Fix: add a per-row --row-ink that flips swap text to cream on dark accents, or lighten the violet fill.
- Suggested command: /impeccable audit (then polish)

**[P1] Finale CTA hierarchy is inverted and fragile.**
- Why it matters: peak-end rule - the loudest object on the final screen is a LinkedIn-brand-blue button while "Start a project" is a dim ghost, and both paths are bare mailto:/LinkedIn with the email address never shown; corporate machines without a mail client hit a silent dead click. The brand's own .btn-solid ships unused.
- Fix: make "Start a project" the violet .btn-solid, demote LinkedIn to ghost, print the email as click-to-copy text near the CTA.
- Suggested command: /impeccable polish

**[P1] Template markers put the site in the "AI made that" bucket.**
- Why it matters: for a portfolio, design IS the product; gradient hero letters, eyebrow grammar on all four sections, two reflex-reject fonts, grain, and the sparkle marquee prove template fluency, not identity. The detector independently flagged the same two headline tells.
- Fix: solid orchid or cream hero letters; keep at most one kicker as a named system and give other sections a different cadence (the "(03)" counter idiom is already better than "PROJECTS"); build the identity outward from the monogram.
- Suggested command: /impeccable bolder (identity), /impeccable typeset (fonts)

**[P2] The scroll price does not match the content bought.**
- Why it matters: ~85% of the mobile page is pinned scrub; projects and contact live in the final ~1.5 screens. Distracted mobile users abandon before the first project. Mobile work rows also layer cover text under pitch text, and the marquee's honest early-stage numbers (168 users, 95 trades) are presented in the visual language of scale.
- Fix: halve the hero to 200vh and manifesto to 150vh; strengthen the cover mask on narrow viewports; use 100svh instead of h-screen so the scroll cue is not behind iOS Safari chrome.
- Suggested command: /impeccable adapt

## Questions to Consider

1. Your honest numbers are 168 users and 95 trades - why borrow the visual language of companies with millions? What would this page look like designed around "early, fast, hungry" instead of pretending at scale?
2. The hero spends 400vh of a visitor's thumb on a rotating letter while the only real evidence of skill - live products - is buried at 75% scroll depth. Would you rather impress a recruiter at screen 3 or lose them there?
3. The 3D monogram is the only thing on this page another AI could not regenerate tomorrow. What happens if you make it the entire identity - and delete the gradient, the eyebrows, and the marquee it currently shares the stage with?
